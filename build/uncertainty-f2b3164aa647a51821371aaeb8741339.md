# Optimization Under Uncertainty

Follow the farmer through four connected examples:

1. [Build the model and compare complete solutions](SP.ipynb): known yields, shared planting, EVPI and VSS.
2. [When VSS is zero](Farmer_Zero_VSS.ipynb): alternative prices with positive value of perfect information.
3. [Sample average approximation](Farmer_SAA.ipynb): 20 synthetic harvests, fresh samples, and repeated experiments.
4. [Conditional value at risk](Farmer_CVaR.ipynb): tail averages, Pyomo, and planting/production as alpha changes.

[Production planning](Production_Planning_Stochastic.ipynb) is recommended additional reading. [Further farmer examples](Farmer_Extensions.ipynb), [advanced sampling](AdvancedTopics.ipynb), and [portfolio risk measures](RiskMeasures.ipynb) provide optional depth.
